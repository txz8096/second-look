Your first message names a screen to build but leaves out who will use it, what success looks like or which tools to use. Those decisions arrive over the next few messages, so the assistant starts building before the brief is complete and some work starts over in a new session.

Evidence and why it matters
Evidence
In the last 4 weeks, 16 of your 25 restartsA new session that opens with mostly the same request as one from the last two days, in the same app. It usually means the first try went off track. were prototype sessions, including 4 Streak settings-screen sessions on Oct 14–15. Set up a skill called ship-checks in Codex and Claude Code. It should run when I say “ship it”, “share this with beta” or ask whether work is ready for others.

When it runs:
- Name the type of work, then run the checks that fit it:
  - Anything beta users will see: list the 3 most likely ways it breaks for a real user, then check each. Use my pr-review skill if it works.

4

Long sessions drift across projects and eat your usage

Claude Code, Codex Likely

Work on one project continues in the same session when you move to another. The next task carries the earlier messages and files with it, increasing the context the assistant has to process and leaving unrelated project details in the session.

Evidence and why it matters
Evidence
In the last 4 weeks, 14 of 72 Claude Code and Codex sessions were very long (past about a novel’s worth of text) and took 61% of your usage. A message in them took 2.6× the usage of your typical one. 2 ran past 60 messages across the onboarding flow, a Streak sync bug and a pricing page.
Why it matters
You reach your limit sooner, and leftovers from the last project can steer the next one.

Ask Codex to set up a rule that offers a handoff and a fresh session when you switch projects.

Saves about a tenth of your Claude Code and Codex usageVery long sessions took 61% of that usage. Starting fresh could cut about a sixth of it.

Before saving, check for an equivalent rule and keep my other instructions. Show me the exact text and where it goes in each app, and wait for my yes. After saving, read it back. For any app you can’t update, give me the text and where to paste it. Tell me which apps to test in a fresh session.

Save a workflow, a checklist or your house style, and the AI follows it every time without being reminded.
